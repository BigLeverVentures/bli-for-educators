# Contributing

Thank you for helping make practical AI education useful and accessible.

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/BigLeverVentures/bli-for-educators/issues) with the lesson title, link, what you tried and where the instructions or result broke down. Suggest a correction if you have one. Share only redacted or fictional material; public issues and pull requests are visible to everyone. You may instead email help@bigleverinstitute.org without private documents or personal details.

For a proposed lesson, explain the audience, useful result, ordinary-question baseline and teaching contribution. For a correction, include the smallest example that demonstrates the problem. A model critique is a lead to investigate, not proof by itself.

Maintainers review source fidelity, rigor, beginner comprehension and burden. Material fixes receive another relevant review. Accepted changes are reconciled with the maintained curriculum before export so the public and community copies do not become competing sources.

This repository is an alpha. Report observed benefits and failures without claiming that one person's result proves effectiveness for everybody. Contribute only material you have permission to share, and identify third-party sources and their terms. New original contributions to our educational material use the repository's Big Lever Educator Teaching-Use License 1.0; verification-code contributions use MIT. Do not relabel third-party work or previously CC-licensed material as exclusively teaching-use: existing independent permissions remain valid. Do not imply Institute endorsement of an adaptation.
