Everyday AI — Practical Examples — Transfer and Optional Reflection

Use the method again

Try a different role or a different email recipient. Keep the method, change the context, and check whether the result still serves that person. Optionally describe what you changed, why it helped, and what you will check next. Keep private source material with you.

Check this changed case

Check that the new version uses the new role or recipient’s facts and does not carry unsupported claims from the earlier draft.

If a correction changes an important fact or behavior, check it again. Reflection is optional: name the change that helped and what you will try next. Keep the useful result; no separate submission or log is required.
