AI Tools and Workflows — Transfer and Optional Reflection

Use the method again

Try briefing a different audience on the same sources. What changes in emphasis, and what factual claims must remain stable? Optionally record one interpretation you revised and the source that changed it. Keep a compact source list and approved synthesis for reuse.

Check this changed case

Trace the briefing’s important claims to the same sources. Change emphasis for the reader without changing the evidence.

If a correction changes an important fact or behavior, check it again. Reflection is optional: name the change that helped and what you will try next. Keep the useful result; no separate submission or log is required.
