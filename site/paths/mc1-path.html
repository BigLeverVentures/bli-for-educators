Everyday AI — Practical Examples — Connected Exercise Path

Begin with evidence and a useful result

Choose the task that matters now: improve a resume for a role you want, OR draft a recurring email you need to send. These are alternatives, not a requirement to do both. Provide your own redacted material or use a clearly labeled sample. Follow the selected exercise through its critique and your decision about revisions.

Carry the work into the next step

For a resume, compare the revised statements with your real experience and the role: unsupported metrics must go. For an email, check the recipient, request and tone before sending it yourself. The shared method is to define the useful result, supply evidence, inspect the proposed approach, and decide what to change after critique.

Check Your Work and Choose What Comes Next explains the basic correction-and-recheck process. Keep the résumé or email’s factual checks here.

Use only the support you need

If a job interview is next, use Interview Coach to rehearse your own answers rather than read a model-written performance. If you will work on the same topic across sessions, use Project Brain; a single email needs no persistent workspace.
